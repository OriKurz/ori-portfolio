A map tracing the story of Villa Epecuén,
a village in Argentina. In 1985, the neighboring saltwater lake flooded the village, and within a few days the entire village lay under 10 meters of saltwater.
It stayed that way for 20 years. In 2009, following a severe drought, the lake began to recede, gradually revealing a ghost town.

The map tells the town’s story across
a 40-year timeline, using the village as a case study of the endless battle between humans and nature.

Working only with data and hard facts, I had to find a way to tell a powerful story about leftovers, and to bring forward what most people would rather forget, or not see at all.

The entire project is based on reliable data and satellite imagery, which served as my anchor throughout
