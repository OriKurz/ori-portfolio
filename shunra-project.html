A branding project for Shunra, a high-end cat food brand based entirely on raw fish dishes. Every dish is made only from fresh fish, fruits and vegetables chosen for cats’ health. Shunra puts your cat first.

Our goal was to bring the sea into every layer of the brand, without ever showing it. A fishing net, stencil-sprayed numbers borrowed from shipping containers, fish market crates and a color palette of sea and port all carry it with them.

We designed packaging for three canned dishes, sashimi and ceviche, and two oils for seasoning them, salmon oil and oyster oil. We also created an illustrative language, posters and an Instagram page that introduce the brand and its flavors. Shunra also comes as merch: matching bandanas for cats and their owners, bags and stickers.
