A series of motion elements for “Touch Grass”, a conference on mental health in times of conflict. The project spans a central event schedule screen, motion graphics promoting the conference on social media, and moving ads for bus stop displays.

Two main questions guided my process: How do we talk about mental health with sensitivity, without being triggering? And how do we talk about PTSD in a bleeding country that is still living through the trauma every day? It was important to me to use natural, handcrafted textures and handwritten elements to bring softness and a human touch to the visuals.

I chose trees as the central visual metaphor for the human mind, and red typography as a quiet reminder of the violence of war.
